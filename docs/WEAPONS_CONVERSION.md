## Weapon Features

### Multi-Mode Weapons
Many weapons support multiple attack modes:
- **1H/2H**: Can be wielded with one or two hands (e.g., Sapergis, Hasta, most spears)
- **1H/2H/Ranged**: Can be used in melee or thrown (e.g., Pilum, Veretum, Soliferrum)
- **Ranged only**: Pure ranged weapons (e.g., bows, crossbows, slings)

### Special Attributes
- **Impaling**: Many weapons can impale (marked with "can impale" in descriptions)
- **Armor Penetration**: Each weapon has an AP (Armor Penetration) value
- **Strike Rank**: Initiative modifier for each weapon
- **Requirements**: STR/DEX requirements properly set from source data

### Pricing
All weapons have proper pricing in lunars (ancient currency) based on source material.

### Organization
Weapons are automatically organized into folders based on their weapon type and skill category:
- Blade weapons (axes, hammers)
- Blunt weapons (maces, clubs)
- Close Combat (daggers, fist weapons)
- Swords
- Spears (including javelins)
- Shields
- Ranged weapons by type
